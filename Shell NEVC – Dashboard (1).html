Go-Live = Go-Live date filled; status, funnel and donut are shown as at the end of the selected period. Sites in several sheets counted once (MSCP first). Deferred sites excluded. Target = deadline month (MSCP); sites with no deadline are assumed on plan in their Go-Live month. Overdue = deadline passed, not Go-Live. Town Council is only recorded for SCP sites. KPI trend uses months reported to Sep; Oct–Dec are not yet final. Time KPI Oct–Dec are forecast. Site location is inferred from the car park code prefix (HDB town) and placed on the five regions of the map; codes with an unknown prefix show as Unlocated. Report progress % = milestones with a date in the tracker (LS Approval to Go-Live, 6 in total); dates later than today show as Scheduled.
